7.5 Savings Plans

📖 Complete Theory

AWS Savings Plans are a flexible pricing model that offers lower prices in exchange for a commitment to a consistent amount of compute usage (measured in $/hour) for a 1-year or 3-year term.

Unlike Reserved Instances, which require you to commit to a specific instance configuration, Savings Plans are tied to your overall compute usage, automatically applying discounts to eligible usage across instance families, regions, operating systems, and tenancies.

7.5.1 Compute Savings Plans

Maximum Flexibility
📖 Complete Theory

Compute Savings Plans is the most flexible of the AWS Savings Plans options. You commit to a consistent hourly spend for a 1- or 3-year term, and AWS applies discounted rates across:

  • Any EC2 instance — all families, all sizes, all regions, all operating systems, all tenancies
  • AWS Fargate — both ECS and EKS on Fargate
  • AWS Lambda — per-request and duration charges

7.5.2 EC2 Instance Savings Plans

Higher Discount, Region-Specific
📖 Complete Theory

EC2 Instance Savings Plans offer the highest discount among AWS Savings Plans — up to 72% off On-Demand rates — in exchange for a commitment to a specific EC2 instance family within a specific AWS Region.

✅ You CAN Change
• Instance Size (m5.large → m5.xlarge)
• Operating System (Linux → Windows)
• Tenancy (Shared → Dedicated)
• Availability Zone (within same Region)
❌ You CANNOT Change
• Instance Family (M5 → C5)
• AWS Region (us-east-1 → eu-west-1)
• Services (EC2 only — no Fargate/Lambda)

📊 Break-even Utilization Analysis:
At rates of 66% (Compute SP) vs 72% (EC2 Instance SP), the break-even utilization is approximately 92% on the EC2 Instance Savings Plan. Below 92% utilization, the Compute Savings Plan delivers equal or better realized savings.

✅ When to Choose EC2 Instance SP:
  • Stable, predictable EC2 workloads — 100% certain about family and region for 1-3 years
  • Pure EC2 environment — no Fargate or Lambda
  • Single-Region concentration — 95%+ of compute permanently in one region
  • No family migration planned — not evaluating Graviton or Intel→ARM migration

⚠️ When to Avoid EC2 Instance SP:
  • Family migration risk — switching families mid-commitment = On-Demand rates
  • Multi-Region workloads — region lock creates exposure
  • Mixed services — EC2 Instance SPs do not cover Fargate or Lambda
  • Uncertain roadmap — if "probably" is the answer, risk is too high
📊 EC2 Instance SP 💰 up to 72% 🔒 region-locked
📝

Key Takeaways

  • Savings Plans are a flexible alternative to Reserved Instances — you commit to an hourly spend ($/hour) rather than a specific instance configuration.

  • Choose Compute Savings Plans for maximum flexibility — covers EC2, Fargate, and Lambda across any region, family, or OS with up to 66% savings.

  • Choose EC2 Instance Savings Plans for higher discounts — up to 72% savings, but tied to a specific instance family in a specific region.

  • Database Savings Plans (new) offer up to 35% savings across 10 database services, available as a 1-year, No Upfront commitment.

  • Savings Plans apply automatically — no manual management of which instance gets the discount.

  • Layer your commitments — use EC2 Instance Savings Plans for stable workloads and Compute Savings Plans for variable or evolving workloads.

  • Savings Plans do not provide capacity reservations — unlike Zonal RIs, Savings Plans are discount-only commitments.

  • Savings Plans do not auto-renew — renew before expiration to maintain coverage.

Review Questions

Q1: What is the fundamental difference between Savings Plans and Reserved Instances?
Answer: Savings Plans commit you to a specific hourly spend ($/hour) that applies automatically across instance families, regions, and services. Reserved Instances commit you to a specific instance configuration (family, size, region, OS, tenancy) that must match the running instances for the discount to apply.
Q2: What are the three main types of Savings Plans?
Answer: The three main types are: Compute Savings Plans (covers EC2, Fargate, Lambda with up to 66% savings), EC2 Instance Savings Plans (covers specific EC2 family/region with up to 72% savings), and Database Savings Plans (covers 10 database services with up to 35% savings, launched December 2025).
Q3: When should you choose a Compute Savings Plan over an EC2 Instance Savings Plan?
Answer: Choose Compute Savings Plans when you need flexibility — if your workload spans multiple instance families, regions, or services (EC2, Fargate, Lambda), or if you expect changes during the commitment term. Choose EC2 Instance Savings Plans when your workload is 100% EC2, concentrated in one family and region, and you are confident it will remain that way for the 1- or 3-year term.
Q4: What is the application order when you have multiple Savings Plans?
Answer: AWS applies Savings Plans in this order: EC2 Instance Savings Plans are applied first (narrowest scope, highest discount), followed by Compute Savings Plans (broader scope), and then any remaining usage is charged at On-Demand rates.
Q5: Can Savings Plans be used for capacity reservations?
Answer: No. Savings Plans are discount-only commitments and do not provide capacity reservations. If you need guaranteed capacity in a specific Availability Zone, you must use Zonal Reserved Instances.
